## Adopt

- Structural variety is a stronger defense against generic output than colour swaps
- Study references by extracting design DNA rather than copying pixels
- Separate surface, type roles, macrostructure, motion, and rhythm during analysis
- Use a pre-emit critique across philosophy, hierarchy, execution, specificity, restraint, and variety
- Lock tokens once the visual direction is selected
- Reject invented metrics and decorative proof
- Treat reference extraction as diagnosis first, build second

## Adapt for Info-stories

- Design DNA fields become: canvas rhythm, title treatment, section topology, card grammar, connector grammar, token roles, density, motion grammar, attribution placement, and visual anchor
- Screenshot/GIF study can estimate rhythm and motion; exact font identification remains uncertain unless the source declares it
- Structural fingerprint is recorded on the story brief and compared when a previous brief is supplied
- Study produces compatible Story House, Visual Style, Story Archetype, and Motion Pattern candidates, not a clone instruction

## Reject

- Marketing-site navigation/footer archetypes and responsive viewport gates
- OKLCH-only output requirements. Existing semantic hex tokens remain supported
- Rules that prohibit every diagrammatic terminal treatment. In this repo a terminal may be an explicit infographic component, but must not pretend to be a real screenshot
- Copying distinctive signature work or marketplace templates
