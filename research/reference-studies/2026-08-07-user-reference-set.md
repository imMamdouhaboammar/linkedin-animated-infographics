# User-attached LinkedIn infographic reference study

Date: 2026-08-07
Source: user-attached reference images and GIFs in the design session
Purpose: extract reusable design DNA for Info-stories without bundling or reproducing the source artwork

## Provenance and limits

The source set is used as public/reference inspiration supplied by the user. The repository stores this diagnosis only, not the source image binaries. Exact font identity is not claimed from screenshots. Motion is described only when it is observable in the uploaded file.

One uploaded `Claude Code for Outbound` GIF is genuinely animated in the supplied asset: 800x1000, 100 frames, 10.0 seconds. Its inter-frame change is visually sparse and the poster frame remains complete. Several other uploaded files use a `.gif` extension but contain one frame in the supplied copy, so their motion cannot be measured from those assets.

## Reference archetypes observed

### Multichannel cheat-sheet board

Observed shape:
- large editorial headline with one accent phrase
- author pill above the title
- dense nine-zone board with numbered colour-coded headers
- repeated card grammar inside zones, but zone dimensions vary
- warm paper texture, terracotta anchor, pastel section identities
- one full-width closing section acts as operational summary

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Signal Sheet**
- Story Archetype: **Framework in One Page** or **Step-by-Step Playbook**
- Story House candidates: **Ember Paper**, **Clay Ledger**
- Motion candidate: **Sequential Highlight** plus a small **Chip / Badge Pulse**

### 7-Figure GTM Funnel

Observed shape:
- vertical routing diagram from traffic sources to capture, enrichment, routing, outreach, and closed deal
- mixed node sizes communicate hierarchy
- connectors carry meaning; boxes do not all have equal weight
- a single dimensional product/brand anchor interrupts an otherwise flat diagram
- portrait and attribution elements act as endpoints, not decoration

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Funnel Board**
- Story Archetype: **From Raw Input to Final Output**
- Story House candidates: **Ember Paper**, **Mist Board**
- Motion candidates: **Connector Draw**, **Active Route Swap**, or **Node Pulse** depending on the takeaway

### Claude Code for Outbound

Observed shape:
- asymmetric dense board rather than a single repeated grid
- top process strip, large terminal zone, central API stack, right setup rail, lower infrastructure and command blocks
- warm paper with dusty coral section fields and near-black terminal surfaces
- content density is high, but major surfaces have distinct jobs
- actual uploaded animation is 100 frames over 10 seconds and remains visually restrained

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Command Canvas** with **Stack Ledger** support
- Story Archetype: **One Prompt, Full Workflow**
- Story House candidates: **Coral Terminal**, **Ember Paper**
- Motion candidates: **Type-On Terminal** plus one restrained secondary pattern such as **Sequential Highlight**

Motion lesson:
- animate a small operational signal, not the entire board
- keep first frame complete
- use long dwell time and small changed-pixel footprint on dense posters

### Build GTM from your terminal

Observed shape:
- headline followed by three large functional columns: find, enrich, launch
- smaller integration modes create a second tier
- provider catalogue becomes a dense but bounded final zone
- section colours are semantic and desaturated rather than decorative

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Tool Catalog** or **Stack Ledger**
- Story Archetype: **Inside the Stack**
- Story House candidates: **Ember Paper**, **Sage Ledger**
- Motion candidate: **Sequential Highlight** by function, not logo-by-logo animation

### Be a 10x GTM Engineer

Observed shape:
- minimal poster with oversized headline, one central visual window, three proof chips, then logo
- large negative space around a single anchor
- background grid/wire treatment supports the anchor without becoming another information layer

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Command Canvas**
- Story Archetype: **The Working Screen**
- Story House candidates: **Mist Board**, **Coral Terminal**
- Motion candidate: **Soft Zoom Focus** or one terminal-state animation

### 19 APIs stack

Observed shape:
- clear horizontal layers from signal through revenue
- each layer has a narrow identity rail plus compact provider pills
- colour distinguishes layer role, while the underlying geometry stays consistent
- high information count without forcing every item into a large card

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Stack Ledger**
- Story Archetype: **Inside the Stack** or **Ecosystem Snapshot**
- Story House candidates: **Ember Paper**, **Sage Ledger**, **Lilac Brief**
- Motion candidates: **Sequential Highlight** or **Connector Draw** when relationships matter

### .md vs Skills vs Hooks vs Subagents vs MCP

Observed shape:
- dark comparison-board concept
- strong title row and fixed row labels
- content is intended to resolve into a matrix rather than independent feature cards
- coral accent against deep navy

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Comparison Grid**
- Story Archetype: **What Each Piece Actually Does**
- Story House: **Navy Grid**
- Motion candidate: row-by-row **Sequential Highlight**, not independent cell motion

### How I make these infographics

Observed shape:
- dark proof collage with four large examples
- the examples themselves are the proof and dominate the composition
- sparse headline, sparse explanatory copy, direct follow CTA
- repeated mascot/sticker texture creates authorship but stays behind the examples

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Proof Mosaic**
- Story Archetype: **Build It Once, Reuse It Often**
- Story House candidates: **Graphite Glow**, **Midnight Operator**
- Motion candidates: **Spotlight Sweep** or **Soft Zoom Focus** on one tile at a time

### Turn any book into a Claude skill

Observed shape:
- numbered vertical story rail at left
- large UI/document examples on the right
- lower comparison/result strip closes the argument
- near-white canvas, soft grid, restrained coral accents and pale step identities

Info-stories mapping:
- Visual Style: **Sequence Board**
- Story Archetype: **Step-by-Step Playbook**
- Story House candidates: **Mist Board**, **Ember Paper**
- Motion candidates: **Sequential Highlight** plus **Card Reveal** only if frame 0 remains complete

## Shared design DNA

### Surface

- warm or cool near-white paper is common; pure flat white is usually softened by texture or faint grid
- dark examples use near-black/navy surfaces with one warm accent
- accent footprint stays small relative to information surfaces
- section identity colours are muted and role-based

### Typography

- one strong headline dominates the first read
- emphasis usually comes from weight or one accent phrase
- body text is compact but structured into short blocks
- micro labels are secondary texture, not where the main takeaway lives
- terminal/mono text appears only where the content genuinely represents commands or code

### Structure

- title zone, core visual anchor, grouped evidence zones, attribution
- the best examples avoid a page made from identical cards
- structural variety comes from zone scale, topology, connector logic, and changing card grammar
- one full-width or oversized zone often resets rhythm after denser modules

### Motion

- movement is sparse relative to total poster area
- sequence, route, or active-state communication is more useful than decorative float
- dense posters benefit from longer dwell and fewer moving targets
- first frame must remain a valid complete infographic

### What Info-stories must not copy

- source names, logos, author portraits, exact copy, product claims, provider lists, or photographs unless separately supplied for the new brief
- exact zone measurements as a fixed template
- a palette-only skin presented as a new style
- a motion pattern inferred from a one-frame upload

## Resulting local coverage

The reference set directly supports these current Visual Styles: **Signal Sheet, Funnel Board, Stack Ledger, Command Canvas, Sequence Board, Comparison Grid, Proof Mosaic, Tool Catalog**. **Story Strip** and **Field Guide** remain locally authored extensions for narrative and reference-heavy use cases not fully represented by the supplied set.
