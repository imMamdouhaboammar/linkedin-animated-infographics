# Story Archetypes

A Story Archetype says what the reader must understand, in what narrative order. It is independent of palette.

## One Prompt, Full Workflow

Slug: `one-prompt-full-workflow`

Required beats: input -> orchestration -> output.

Recommended density: 3-7 content units.

## From Raw Input to Final Output

Slug: `raw-input-to-final-output`

Required beats: raw-input -> stages -> final-output.

Recommended density: 4-8 content units.

## The 30-Day Breakdown

Slug: `thirty-day-breakdown`

Recommended density: 4-8 content units.

## Inside the Stack

Slug: `inside-the-stack`

Recommended density: 5-9 content units.

## What Each Piece Actually Does

Slug: `what-each-piece-does`

## Build It Once, Reuse It Often
