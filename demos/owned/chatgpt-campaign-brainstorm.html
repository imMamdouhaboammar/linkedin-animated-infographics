Prompt sequence · 5 steps

ChatGPT's ideas are only as good as the decisions behind them.

From messy context to one direction worth testing

01CONTEXT

Give it the real numbers before asking for ideas.

“Product, audience, budget, deadline, and what the top competitor is running. What three behavior shifts should this campaign realistically produce?”
02ANGLES

Force range before the first idea becomes the only idea.

“Give me 8 campaign angles from that context. Each one needs a different emotional trigger and mechanism, not a reworded version of the same idea.”
03STRESS TEST

Make it argue against its own shortlist.

“Take the top 3 angles. For each, argue where it fails with this audience, this budget, or this timeline.”
04VERDICT

One ranked reason beats five unranked options.

“Rank the 3 by expected impact, then execution risk. Tell me which you'd greenlight and the one reason that decided it.”
05BRIEF

This is the version a designer or copywriter can actually work from.

“Turn the winner into a one-page brief: objective, audience, key message, tone, 3 directions, and the KPI that proves it worked.”

Brainstorm the decision before you brainstorm the idea.
